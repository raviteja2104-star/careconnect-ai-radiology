# Clinical Training Checklist

This checklist must be signed off by the Clinical Lead prior to Go-Live.

## 1. Reception Staff
- [ ] Understands the Patient Registration flow.
- [ ] Can successfully book an OPD appointment.
- [ ] Knows how to invoke the "Downtime Procedure" (paper fallback).

## 2. Doctors & Nurses
- [ ] Can locate the daily appointment queue.
- [ ] Knows how to initiate a Telemedicine video session.
- [ ] Can successfully input and submit an E-Prescription.
- [ ] Understands the ABDM Consent approval workflow.

## 3. Billing & Finance
- [ ] Can generate an invoice.
- [ ] Can verify a Razorpay/Stripe payment status.
- [ ] Understands the end-of-day reconciliation report.

## 4. Support Procedures
- [ ] All staff know the internal phone extension for IT Support.
- [ ] All staff know how to identify the "Clinical Champions" on the floor.
